# Demo video script: Pretty Smart

**Pretty looks. Smart picks.**

Target length: about 2:45 (Devpost allows 1–3 minutes). Record in **demo mode**
(`DEMO_MODE=1`), so every step replays instantly and costs nothing. Tag **@YouCam API**
in the post.

Numbers in [brackets] come from your final recorded session: read them off the
screen when recording, don't use these placeholders.

---

## 0:00–0:15 · The problem

**On screen:** a look photo, then a product page with a $40+ lipstick.

> I see a look I love: a friend's photo, a creator's post. I want it, and I
> want to spend smart. But a shade that matches in the tube can look totally
> different on my face. So how do I know what really works on me?

## 0:15–0:35 · Tab 1: me, in this look

**On screen:** My photos tab → **Show me in this look** → lips-only preview → tick **Show the full look**.

> Pretty Smart starts with two photos: my selfie and the look. YouCam's Makeup
> Transfer puts the whole look on my face. Then it finds my match, one product at
> a time, starting with the lips.

## 0:35–1:05 · Tab 2: lipstick, tried on my lips

**On screen:** **Find my match** → Lipstick tab → "My smart pick" box → one shade card →
switch **Close-ups / Full face**.

> It tries the closest shades on my lips with YouCam's makeup try-on, the splurge
> and the smart version of each, and measures every result on my face, not in the
> tube. My smart pick is [shade]: a [score]% match, and the $[smart] one is a true
> twin of the $[splurge] one on me, so I keep $[kept]. And when it isn't a twin,
> it tells me honestly that the splurge is worth it.

**Optional line:** "It also remembers how colors shift on my lips, so my next look
needs fewer try-ons."

## 1:05–1:25 · Tab 3: blush

**On screen:** Blush tab → **Match my blush too** → smart pick card.

> Blush is harder: it blends into skin. So we measure only the color it *adds* to
> my cheeks, compared with my forehead in the same photo. My blush: [shade],
> [score]% match, and I keep $[kept].

## 1:25–2:05 · Tab 4: hair and brows

**On screen:** Hair & brows → **The look's hairstyle** → pick a hair color that is
a true dupe → pick a fake one (for example **Auburn**) showing ✗ → the ★ brow
suggestion → **Use in your look** → the picture on the left updates.

> The look includes the hair. YouCam's Hairstyle Generator gives me this hairstyle
> and color. Want a different color? It tries the salon color and the everyday box
> dye on my hair: [twin color] is a true twin, so I keep $[kept], but this Auburn
> box dye isn't, so the salon color is worth it there. A big hair change can make
> natural brows look off, so it suggests a brow shade and checks the $[pencil]
> pencil against the $[gel] gel on my brows.

## 2:05–2:25 · Tab 5: my look

**On screen:** My look tab → the **Twinning ✨** reveal plays (the look photo and me slide in,
lean toward each other, sparkles, "Same look. I kept $[kept].") → **Love it 💖** (confetti)
→ **Get my look**. Optional: **Save as video** to show the shareable clip.

> Here's the look I loved, next to me in it, all combined on my own photo:
> lipstick, blush, hair color and brows for $[total], and I kept $[kept].
> Pretty looks, smart picks.

## 2:25–2:45 · For retailers: the Dupe Audit

**On screen:** For retailers → Dupe Audit → demo report grid → the synthetic / real labels.

> Retailers get the same proof before they market a dupe: test shade pairs on
> several faces, including AI-generated synthetic faces from YouCam's image
> generator, clearly labeled, and see where a dupe fails, for example only on
> deeper lip tones.

## 2:45 · Close

**On screen:** title card.

> Pretty Smart. Pretty looks. Smart picks.
> Built with the YouCam API: Makeup Transfer, Makeup try-on, Hairstyle
> Generator, Hair Color and AI Image Generator.

## Short text (Devpost tagline / social post)

**Tagline:** Pretty looks. Smart picks.

**One-liner (under 200 characters):**
Try any look on my own face, find the lipstick, blush, hair color and brows that
match it, proven on my face, and keep the difference.

**Elevator pitch:**
A shade that matches in the tube can look different on your face. Pretty Smart puts
a look you love on your own face with YouCam's Makeup Transfer and Hairstyle
Generator, then tries the splurge and the smart version of each product on you and
measures every result on your face. You see your smart pick, an honest "true twin
or not" for every lower-priced version, how much you keep, and the whole look
combined on your photo with one cart. Saving feels like a smart choice, not
settling. Retailers get a Dupe Audit to test shade pairs on real and synthetic
faces before marketing a dupe.

**YouCam APIs used:** Makeup Transfer, Makeup VTO (lip color, blush, eyebrows),
AI Hairstyle Generator v2.1, AI Hair Color, AI Image Generator.
